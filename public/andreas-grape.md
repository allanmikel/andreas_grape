## Summary

Andreas Grape is a Stockholm-based serial entrepreneur and angel investor with
25+ years of building, scaling, and exiting companies. He is the co-founder of
**Nordic Angels** (founded 2020 with Ash Pournouri), the institutional layer —
the "operating system" — for the Nordic private capital market, connecting
angels, founders, and the capital that scales them. He co-owns **Angel House**,
a private members club for angels and creatives that opened in Stockholm in
2025, and is building cross-border private-capital infrastructure across the
Nordics.

## Background (chronological)

- **2001** — Early investor in **Academic Work**, now Scandinavia's largest
  staffing company. The bet that defined an asset class and a career.
- **2008–2021** — Built an IT consulting company from scratch, scaled it, and
  exited in 2021. 13 years that taught the operating playbook.
- **2020** — Co-founded **Nordic Angels** with Ash Pournouri during the
  pandemic to unite business angels and empower the Nordic startup ecosystem.
- **2025** — Opened **Angel House**, a private members club for angels and
  creatives, as the operational base for the Nordic capital community.
- **Now** — Building the operating system for the Nordic private capital market.
